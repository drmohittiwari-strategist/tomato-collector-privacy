# Privacy Policy for Tomato Collector

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Tomato Collector ("the game") is a free game published by Mohit Tiwari.

## Information we collect
The game does not collect, store on a server, share, or sell any personal information. It does not require an account or login.

## Data stored on your device
The game saves your high score in a small file on your own device. This data stays on your device and is never sent to us or to anyone else. It is deleted when you uninstall the game.

## Permissions
The game does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, or files.

## Advertising and analytics
The game currently contains no advertising and uses no analytics or tracking tools. If this changes in a future version, this policy will be updated before the change is released.

## Children
The game does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy
If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
